CHASSIS · AIR SYSTEM

Air suspension / air bags

Air springs support the chassis and maintain ride height. A leaning tractor or trailer, repeated compressor cycling, or audible hissing can indicate an air leak or ride-height problem.

Quick checks
  • Park safely on level ground and secure the vehicle.
  • Compare ride height left-to-right and inspect bags for cracks, folds or obvious damage.
  • Listen for leaks around the bag, fittings and height-control valve.
  • Do not crawl beneath an air-suspended vehicle unless it is mechanically supported.
Next action

If a bag is damaged, the chassis is sitting low, or pressure will not hold, keep the vehicle out of service and have the air-suspension fault repaired. Do not rely on air suspension alone to support the vehicle.
